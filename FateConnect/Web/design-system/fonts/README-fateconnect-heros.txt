FateConnect Heros

fateconnect-heros-regular.woff2 and fateconnect-heros-bold.woff2 are a modified
version of TeX Gyre Heros 2.004 (texgyreheros-regular.otf and texgyreheros-bold.otf,
from https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre). The fonts were renamed to "FateConnect Heros"
and subset to Latin (U+0000-017F and common punctuation and symbols), then converted
to WOFF2. Nothing else was changed.
